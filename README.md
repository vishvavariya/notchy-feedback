# Notchy — free Dynamic Island for Mac

**[notchy.dev](https://notchy.dev)** · [Download](https://notchy.dev/download/) · [Features](https://notchy.dev/features/) · [Changelog](https://notchy.dev/changelog/)

Notchy turns the MacBook notch into a Dynamic Island: one fluid surface that springs open for music, timers, AirPods, battery and system HUDs, then settles back out of the way. It is free — no tiers, no trial, no licence key — and native SwiftUI, idling around 0.1% CPU.

- **Face Unlock** — look at your locked Mac and it opens. On-device, with a liveness check.
- **Now Playing** — album art, controls and per-app volume in the notch.
- **Clipboard history and a file shelf** — drag files into the notch, paste anything you copied.
- **Stock ticker, timers, calendar** — live activities that morph in and out.
- **Window snapping, OCR, quick search** — the small utilities you'd otherwise buy separately.
- **AI usage tracking** — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and more — spend and limits at a glance.
- **Works without a notch** — Mac mini, iMac and external displays get a floating island.

Runs on **macOS 13 Ventura or later**, Intel and Apple silicon.

## Install

```sh
brew install --cask vishvavariya/notchy/notchy
```

or grab the signed, notarized DMG from [notchy.dev/download](https://notchy.dev/download/). Notchy updates itself.
